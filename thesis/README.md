# Thesis Writing Materials

This directory will contain materials directly related to writing the master's thesis.

The final format has not yet been selected.

Possible future contents include:

```text
thesis/
├── chapters/
├── figures/
├── tables/
└── bibliography/
```

Do not create these directories until they are needed.

## Important distinction

Research documentation belongs primarily in:

`docs/`

Final thesis text and publication-ready figures belong in:

`thesis/`

The goal is to keep research notes separate from final academic writing.
